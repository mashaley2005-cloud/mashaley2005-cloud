My story

I am a director and screenwriter drawn to stories about intimacy, memory, loneliness, and human relationships. I have been writing stories and making films since childhood.

Alongside my own directing practice, I have built experience across different areas of film and content production. I work as an editor and content creator, collaborating with creators whose audiences reach hundreds of thousands of followers. In 2024, I worked as a content maker for the international marketing agency Epiic.

I have also worked on set as an assistant director on advertising projects for Yandex and IQOO, as well as on music videos, clips, and short-form productions. Working in different roles has taught me how to move between the creative and practical sides of filmmaking. I understand how an idea develops from the first line of a script to the final cut.
